### Take a Day Off

- `default_days`: the number the dialog starts on. `1` = tomorrow.
- `include_intraday_learning`: also move cards partway through today's learning
  steps, so a week off leaves literally nothing waiting. Turn this off if you'd
  rather half-learned cards greet you the moment you come back.
- `backup_first`: force a collection backup before the shift. Leave this on.
